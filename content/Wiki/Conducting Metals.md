[[Model of Conduction]]

- In conducting metals in equilibrium:
$$\begin{gathered}
\mathbf{r} \in V \implies\mathbf{E}_{\text{tot}} = \mathbf{E}_{\text{metal}} + \mathbf{E}_{\text{ext}} = 0 \\
\mathbf{r} \in V \implies \psi \equiv const. \\
\mathbf{r} \in \partial V \implies \rho \equiv 0
\end{gathered}$$
- The electrostatic field right outside a conductor (due to [[Maxwell Equations#^7d927f]] ):
$$\sigma = \frac{E_{n}}{4\pi} = \frac{\mathbf{E}\cdot \mathbf{\hat{n}}}{4\pi} = -\frac{1}{4\pi}\frac{ \partial \psi }{ \partial n } $$
- The pressure on the surface of the conductor (using [[Energy Stored in EM Field]]):
$$\frac{dU}{dV} = u = \frac{1}{8\pi}\mathbf{E}^2 = 2\pi\sigma^2 \implies P = 2\pi\sigma^2$$
