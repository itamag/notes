[[Approximation Methods in QM]]

In theory, it's straightforward to calculate to first and second order corrections to the eigen energies and states. 

In practice, you need to represent the perturbation $V$ over the original eigenbasis, which is not pretty. Most of the work and gotchas are around working around it. The rest of the work is working with [[Perturbation Theory#Time-Independent Degenerate QM Perturbation Theory|degenerate subspaces]].
